// SECURITY ARCHITECTURE

Zero Trust Framework

⬡
Never Trust. Always Verify.
WikkiShield is built on the Zero Trust security model — every piece of content, every link, every file, and every message is treated as potentially hostile until proven safe. No implicit trust is granted based on location, sender, or familiarity. Every scan is independent. Every session is isolated.
🔍
Verify Everything
Every URL, file, message, and email is scanned independently — regardless of who sent it, how familiar it looks, or what platform it came from. A message from your closest contact can still carry a phishing link.
🔒
Least Privilege Access
Your personal data is stored in your own isolated, encrypted browser store — keyed to your account alone. No scan data is shared between users. No centralised profile. Your threat history belongs only to you.
📡
Assume Breach
WikkiShield operates under the assumption that threats are already inside your network, your inbox, and your device. Our offline heuristic engine works even without internet — because attackers don't wait for you to connect.
🧠
Continuous Validation
Trust is never assumed for longer than one session. Re-authentication is required for sensitive modules. The Safety Score quiz is designed to be retaken monthly — because your threat exposure changes as your habits change.
📊
Micro-Segmentation
Each WikkiShield module operates independently. A breach in one module cannot cascade to others. Scan results, reports, simulator scores, and personal data are stored in separate isolated buckets per user per module.
🌍
2,000+ Threat Dataset
WikkiShield's detection engine is trained on 2,000+ labelled threat samples spanning government portals, banking, fintech, education, energy, AI tools, Web3, SCADA systems, national procurement, NGO grant fraud, and more. Every Zero Trust principle requires real intelligence to enforce — this is ours.

Defence Playbook

🎣
You clicked a phishing link. What now?
Act fast — the first 15 minutes are critical. Follow these steps in order.
STEP 01
DO NOW
📵
Disconnect Immediately
Cut off internet access to stop data exfiltration.
Turn off Wi-Fi and mobile data
Enable Airplane Mode if needed
Do NOT click any more links on the page
STEP 02
DO NOW
🔑
Change Your Passwords
Assume all credentials entered are compromised.
Change email password first
Change banking and payment passwords
Enable 2FA on all important accounts
STEP 03
DO NOW
🏦
Alert Your Bank
If financial info was entered, freeze accounts immediately.
Call bank's fraud hotline
Request temporary card freeze
Monitor for suspicious transactions
STEP 04
SOON
🔍
Scan Your Device
Check for malware that may have been installed.
Run full antivirus scan
Check browser extensions for unknowns
Upload suspicious files to WikkiShield
STEP 05
SOON
📋
Document Everything
Preserve evidence for reporting and recovery.
Screenshot the phishing page (safely)
Note the URL and time of incident
Save any emails or messages received
STEP 06
LATER
🚨
Report the Threat
Help protect others by reporting the phishing site.
Report to WikkiShield Community Feed
Report to Google Safe Browsing
Notify your email provider
🦠
Malware detected on your device. Act now.
Don't panic — systematic action will contain the damage. Follow these steps.
STEP 01
DO NOW
📵
Isolate the Device
Prevent the malware from spreading to other devices or accounts.
Disconnect from all networks
Do not connect USB drives
Avoid logging into accounts from this device
STEP 02
DO NOW
🛡
Boot Into Safe Mode
Limit what malware can execute during your cleanup.
Restart device into Safe Mode
Run antivirus from safe mode only
Disable startup items you don't recognise
STEP 03
DO NOW
🗑
Remove the Malware
Quarantine and delete the threat using trusted tools.
Run full Malwarebytes / Windows Defender scan
Quarantine all flagged files
Upload suspect files to WikkiShield
STEP 04
SOON
🔑
Change All Credentials
Keyloggers or spyware may have captured passwords.
Use a CLEAN device to change passwords
Enable 2FA everywhere
Revoke unknown app authorisations
STEP 05
SOON
💾
Restore from Backup
If malware persists, a clean restore is safest.
Restore OS from clean backup
Reinstall from scratch if no backup
Only restore personal files — not apps
STEP 06
LATER
🧠
Learn & Prevent
Understand how the malware entered to prevent recurrence.
Identify the infection vector
Install reputable antivirus software
Keep OS and apps fully updated

You've been targeted by a scam. Don't panic.
Whether money was lost or not, these steps protect you now and in the future.
STEP 01
DO NOW
🏦
Stop Any Payments
If money hasn't moved yet, freeze accounts immediately.
Call your bank's fraud line now
Cancel any pending transfers
Block the scammer's account number
STEP 02
DO NOW
✋
Cut Contact
Block all communication channels with the scammer.
Block phone number and WhatsApp
Block email address
Do not respond — silence is best
STEP 03
DO NOW
📋
Document Everything
Evidence is critical for recovery and prosecution.
Screenshot all conversations
Save all phone numbers and emails
Note all amounts and dates
STEP 04
SOON
🚔
Report to Authorities
File reports with relevant agencies for potential recovery.
Report to your national cybercrime authority
File a police report with evidence
Report to your bank's fraud department
STEP 05
SOON
🔐
Secure Your Identity
Scammers may use your data for further attacks.
Check for accounts opened in your name
Update passwords on all platforms
Enable 2FA on financial accounts
STEP 06
LATER
💙
Seek Support
Scams are emotionally traumatic. You're not alone.
Talk to someone you trust
Contact consumer protection organisations
Share your experience to warn others
🔓
Your data may have been breached. Act immediately.
A data breach exposes personal info. The faster you act, the more you can limit.
STEP 01
DO NOW
🔑
Change Compromised Passwords
Start with the breached service, then related accounts.
Change the breached account password
Change any accounts using the same password
Use a unique password for every account
STEP 02
DO NOW
📱
Enable 2FA Everywhere
Two-factor authentication blocks attackers even with your password.
Enable SMS or authenticator app 2FA
Prioritise email and banking accounts
Use Google Authenticator or Authy
STEP 03
DO NOW
🏦
Monitor Financial Accounts
Check for unauthorised transactions immediately.
Check all bank and card statements
Set up SMS alerts for transactions
Request a new card number if needed
STEP 04
SOON
🔍
Check Breach Scope
Understand exactly what data was exposed.
Visit haveibeenpwned.com
Read the official breach notification
Identify if payment or ID data leaked
STEP 05
SOON
⚠
Watch for Follow-up Attacks
Breaches are often followed by targeted phishing.
Be extra suspicious of emails post-breach
Don't click links in "security alert" emails
Go directly to websites by typing the URL
STEP 06
LATER
📦
Use a Password Manager
Never reuse passwords again — let a manager do the work.
Install Bitwarden or 1Password
Generate unique passwords for every site
Store your master password securely offline

You found AI-generated misinformation. Fight back.
AI fake news spreads faster than corrections. Every report helps slow the tide.
STEP 01
DO NOW
🧪
Verify with WikkiShield
Confirm it's AI-generated before taking action.
Paste text or upload image to Detector
Note the AI probability score
Screenshot the WikkiShield result
STEP 02
DO NOW
🔇
Don't Amplify
Even debunking can spread false content further.
Do not share — even to "warn" friends
Do not screenshot and repost
Share the debunk, not the original
STEP 03
DO NOW
📌
Cross-Check the Claim
Verify with multiple trusted sources before concluding.
Search the claim on Reuters, BBC, NAN
Check fact-checking sites like Dubawa
Look for primary source references
STEP 04
SOON
🚩
Report to Platform
Use built-in tools to flag the content for review.
Use platform "Report" feature
Select "Misinformation" or "False Info"
Include your WikkiShield evidence
STEP 05
SOON
📝
Submit to WikkiShield
Help our AI learn and improve detection accuracy.
Submit via Incident Report form
Include original source link
Mark as AI Misinformation type
STEP 06
LATER
🎓
Educate Your Network
Train others to spot AI-generated content.
Share the WikkiShield Scam Simulator
Teach others the 5 red flags of deepfakes
Share WikkiShield with your community
